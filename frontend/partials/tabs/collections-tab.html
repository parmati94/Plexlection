Overriding a collection by hand

A Plex smart collection has no "add to collection" action — its membership comes from the filter. To override, add one of these labels to an item in Plex:

  • plexlection:<slug>:pin — force it in
  • plexlection:<slug>:veto — force it out

Plexlection never writes or removes those two, so your choices survive every sync.
